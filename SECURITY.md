# Security policy

## Scope
SOLSplit program `5M5cpnesJ6PUzAu3kYeGk6GaiEsL2WgHroLE2FHNTP1R` (Solana devnet) and its demo at https://xspence.co.uk/solsplit/.
The program is a devnet demonstration. It holds no real-value funds and has not been audited.

## Reporting
Email info@xspence.co.uk with a description, affected component and reproduction steps.
Please do not open public issues for vulnerabilities. Expect an acknowledgement within 7 days.

## Out of scope
Devnet SOL faucet limits, rate limiting of the public demo, and third-party services (RPC, Explorer).
